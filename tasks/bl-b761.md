Adoption evidence (a downstream consumer, first day live): 29 of 30 closes paid the full local gate; 6 of 7 remote pre-builds built candidate trees nothing ever closed on. Root cause is not mechanism — it is that joining the queue is a conscious act. \`enqueue\` demands the agent (a) know the queue exists, (b) know THIS commit is its last, and (c) forgo an immediately-available \`bl close\` to wait on a builder. Every incentive points at skipping it, and fail-open makes skipping invisible.

The reframe: nobody needs to know which commit is the last one. Seal EVERY quiet tip; the last commit's seal is the one that survives. Eviction is already free by design ("any new commit unseals you; re-enqueue is requeue-at-bottom"), so a wrong seal costs at most one wasted build.

The change, all inside the speculator pass (\`speculate_run::run\`):

1. A new ADOPT stage at PASS END: enumerate \`refs/heads/work/*\`; every branch not currently sealed at its tip gets \`merging/<id>\` planted at the LISTED sha (not re-resolved — a branch a concurrent close deletes mid-pass yields a tag that derives unsealed and is swept next pass, instead of an error arm no test can reach). Report line: \`adopted <id> <tip>\`.
2. Adoption at pass END is the debounce: a tip must survive one full inter-pass interval untouched before the walk will build it. No clock is consulted — quiescence is measured in passes, so operator clock policy (smeared commit dates included) cannot break it.
3. Sweep + adopt in one pass IS requeue-at-bottom for a moved tip: the walk dequeues the unsealed entry, the adopt stage re-seals the new tip with a fresh taggerdate.
4. Queue position among same-pass adoptees: taggerdate, refname tie-break — already the queue's ordering rule; for-each-ref's refname order makes adoption deterministic.
5. An agent-planted seal (explicit \`enqueue\`) still works and still outranks: its taggerdate predates the pass that would have adopted it.

Not in scope: no new verb, no new flag, no signature change to \`run\`. The driver's cadence is the only knob, and it is not a knob in balls — it is whoever invokes the pass.

Doc amendment rides along: docs/design/bl-24e7-speculative-merge-queue.md "Unsealed branches are stale snapshots; do not speculate on them" gains the adoption clause (the pass may SEAL a quiet unsealed tip; it still never BUILDS an unsealed one), and the scheduling note "agents invoke a pass when idle" widens to an ambient driver.
