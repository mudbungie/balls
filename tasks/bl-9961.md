CONVERGED DIRECTION (maintainer dialogue 2026-07-07): the diagnosis is "two fields (subject + body) crammed through one flag via git's first-line convention" — correct. But the fix is NOT `-t title` / `-b body`. §5 already says the commit subject is ALWAYS the ball's title — deliberately NO override — so `git log` readers never see balls-flavored subjects. So there is NO title to pass at close: it IS the ball's title (edit it with `bl update` if wrong). The only free field at close is the BODY narration.

FIX (subtraction): `-m` (the close narration) is BODY-ONLY, and the delivery-squash subject is ALWAYS `<ball title> [bl-id]` — exactly what the STORE seal already does (Message::render: subject = title, `-m` = body). Adding a `-t` would REINTRODUCE the subject override §5 explicitly removed. So the flag collapses to ONE field (body), and the mangling becomes structurally impossible.

THE BUG TODAY: delivery_message::compose (bl-b9a6) treats `-m` (override_msg) as a FULL subject+body override (highest precedence), and with_marker() inserts `[bl-id]` after the first PHYSICAL line. A body-only `-m` (the natural thing, given the project's "write rich commit bodies" rule) makes the whole first PARAGRAPH the delivery subject with the tag jammed mid-sentence. The STORE seal does NOT do this — only the delivery squash — so the inconsistency is internal to one op.

RECONCILE bl-b9a6 (the reason override exists — carry rich work-branch context onto the squash, not just the ball title): put that context in the BODY under the title subject, not as a subject replacement. Then work-branch messages AND `-m` are both body; the subject is always the title.

RECURRING papercut; hit again on bl-98ba's delivery 2026-07-07 (fixed by amending in a throwaway `git worktree add <p> main`). Recommend the subtraction fix over documenting it, since documenting has not stopped it recurring.
