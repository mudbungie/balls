The one behavior change of docs/design/bl-7b71-nested-delivery.md. Needs the mechanism ball first — the flip is meaningless until a target ref exists.

Today `--subtask-of E` is sugar for `--parent E --blocks claim`: E cannot be CLAIMED until this child closes. Under nesting that inverts — the epic is claimed first (or never), children deliver into work/<E>, and E cannot CLOSE until they close. So the sugar must mint `--parent E --blocks close`.

Self-migrating by construction: pre-existing subtasks carry a claim-gate and no close-gate, so old epics keep flat delivery untouched and nothing needs converting. The known wart is that two identically-spelled epics then behave differently by creation date. The design leaves that UNDECIDED on purpose — decide it when an old epic actually bites, and if it needs converting, prime is the established home for version-skew convergence (bl-18bf). Do not build a converter speculatively.

Doc sweep is the bulk of this ball: every place claim-gate semantics are taught — SKILL.md, skill/create.md (its dependency-model section spells out "E cannot be claimed until this closes" and the reason it keeps epics out of `list -s ready`; that reasoning changes), skill/close.md, import. Also raise the stale-checkout caveat to its general altitude: SKILL.md currently says a non-bare root is stale after a close because delivery moves MAIN by plumbing; the general rule is that a delivery advances a ref by plumbing and never touches a checkout of it — main is the instance, and a claimed epic worktree is the new one.
