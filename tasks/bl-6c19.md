Found during bl-1fc4 (which added require_id_shape to every id-taking flag on create/update): import_stream.rs checks id::is_valid on the record's own id, but parent and blockers[].id deserialize unchecked — the same garbage-parent hole bl-1fc4 closed, reachable via a different door. Left alone deliberately in that fix because docs/architecture.md §9 says import is verbatim reproduction, not a transition. The tension to resolve: uniform shape validation (a graph with garbage ids is broken everywhere downstream) vs §9's round-trip fidelity doctrine (import must reproduce what export said, including historical records minted under other schemes). Candidate reframes: validate shape but not liveness (matching bl-1fc4's line); or keep verbatim and declare garbage-in-garbage-out for import in §9 explicitly. Deliverable: the decision recorded in §9 plus whichever validation (or explicit non-validation) it implies.
