Observed during bl-d3fa: cargo doc emits 68 pre-existing warnings, nearly all private intra-doc links (e.g. crate::target::derive) — house style links to private items, rustdoc warns. Consequence: a genuinely broken doc link cannot be spotted. Decide the cheap end: either pass --document-private-items in the doc invocation the repo blesses (Makefile/CI, making the links resolve and the warnings vanish), or switch house style to code-font-no-link for private items. Do NOT add a warning allowlist. Whichever way, the deliverable includes making the blessed doc build warning-clean so future breaks are visible.
